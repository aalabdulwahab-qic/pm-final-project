## Persona & problem
- **Role:** Venue decision-makers and the analysts who support them. Their goal is to get one specific number (attendance, per-capita spend, ride downtime) that they can act on or defend, under time pressure. The friction confirmed in reconciliation is that they find numbers but can't tell which is official, so they fall back to a personal spreadsheet, an Excel export, or a colleague. The caveat is that my Decision-maker row (58% certified assets, low requests) contradicts this, so check it against real segment data.
- **Goal:** Ultimate goal: Get a number they can act on or defend, quickly, without a workaround.
- **Friction:** Main barrier: The platform can't take them from a question to one answer they can verify. They can't find the right asset, and when they do find something, they can't tell whether to trust it.Moments of misery by persona Priya (UXR-01), the findability barrier  Moment: Before the morning briefing, she needs today's attendance against capacity. She scrolls through dashboards for twenty minutes, closes the portal without an answer, and goes back to her own spreadsheet. Why it hurts: The deadline is fixed, and a six-year user still leaves empty-handed. Related defects: BUG-2042 (High), where search matches only exact report names and venue naming is inconsistent. Marcus (UXR-02), the trust barrier  Moment: He finds three dashboards showing "per-capita spend." Each gives a different number, and nobody can tell him which is official. In his words: "I don't need more dashboards, I need to know which one to trust." Why it hurts: He found the data, but he still can't use it, because he can't defend any of the numbers. Related defects: BUG-2058 (Critical), BUG-2061 (High), BUG-2080 (Medium).
- **Problem you are solving:** Decision-makers and analysts who search for a key metric find several conflicting versions with no signal of which one is official, so they can't defend any of them and leave the platform to build their own.

## Outcome & metrics
- **Strategic outcome:** Behaviour change: users take the official number from the platform, and stop exporting, copying or asking colleagues. Retention and churn: fewer users abandon the platform after a failed first attempt, which should improve Mo. 0→1 retention (verify against the heatmap). - **Primary success metric:** Share of sessions that check trust info and then use the asset, currently about 39% (100% minus the 61% post-check loss). Proposed target: 50% or more. Supporting signals are a falling duplicate-request count (from 46 a month) and a falling export or local-copy rate among decision-makers and analysts.
- **Guardrail metric:** Search → asset-opened rate must stay at or above 52%, with time to find an asset no worse than 18 min. These protect findability while you change how trust is shown. A second guardrail is Mo. 0→1 retention for existing cohorts, which must not fall below its current level.
